About Tanzania Safari

Who We Are

A Tanzania-based team, built around the bush

We are a Tanzania-based safari planning company working with guides, drivers and camps who live and work in the landscapes we send travellers into. Our role is to translate that local knowledge into an itinerary that actually fits how you want to travel.

We don't run every safari the same way. Each trip is planned around the season, the wildlife movement at that time of year, and what you personally want to get out of the days you have.

Our Approach

Safari Philosophy

Three ideas guide how we plan every trip.

Follow the Wildlife

Routes are shaped around where animals actually are that month, not a fixed loop.

Plan Around You

Pace, comfort level and interests shape the itinerary, not a set package.

Travel Responsibly

We favour parks, conservancies and camps that reinvest in conservation and local communities.

Commitment

Responsible Tourism

Tanzania's parks and conservancies depend on tourism that respects the land and the people who live alongside it. We work with operators who follow park guidelines, employ local staff, and support community-run conservancies where safari revenue funds local services and wildlife protection.
